Getting started

Add a separate app account.

Start with a compatible app already installed on your Android phone. Keep the original app installed while setting up its separate instance.

  1. Open Phantom and choose Add app. Find the app you want to use with another account.
  2. Create the instance. Follow the app’s on-screen setup, then return to the home screen.
  3. Sign in inside the new instance. Use the account you want for this space and complete any sign-in checks required by that app.

Compatibility and sign-in requirements are controlled partly by the other app. Some apps may not work in a separate space. Contact us if you are unsure.

Troubleshooting

When an app does not behave as expected.

  • Check that both Phantom and the original app are up to date in Google Play.
  • Confirm your internet connection if the app needs to sign in or load online content.
  • Restart Phantom and try opening the instance again.
  • For delayed notifications, review Android’s notification and battery settings for Phantom. Available settings vary by device.

Keep your data in mind. Clearing app storage or removing an instance can erase its local data. Before doing either, make sure important information is saved or synced through the original app’s own account service.

If the problem continues, send us the details below instead of repeatedly reinstalling.

Purchases

Manage a Google Play subscription.

  1. Open Google Play with the Google account that made the purchase.
  2. Tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  3. Choose your app subscription to view its billing details or follow Google Play’s cancellation instructions.

Deleting an app does not cancel its Google Play subscription. A website support message does not cancel a subscription either.

For the current Google Play steps, see Google Play’s subscription help. For an app-specific issue, contact us without sharing full payment information.

Contact support

A useful report gets us to the right problem.

  • The app or feature you were using.
  • Your phone model and Android version.
  • Your Phantom version.
  • What you expected, what happened, and the steps to reproduce it.
  • The exact error message, if there is one.

Remove passwords, one-time codes, and private information from your report or screenshots.
