Good places to start

Answers before you ask.

How do I add another account?

Use Add app to create a separate instance of a compatible installed app, then open it and sign in. Read the setup guide.

An app will not open. What should I send support?

Tell us the app’s name, your phone model, Android version, and Phantom version. Include the error message and steps to reproduce it. Crop private information from screenshots.

How do I manage a subscription?

Subscriptions purchased through Google Play are managed in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. Read the subscription guide.
